Everything so far

Every picture is plain text: several hundred thousand characters, each as small as one screen pixel. Each letter is chosen for its weight and for its shape, so the ink sits where the light falls and the strokes run along the edges. Zoomed out, the page shows the ink each letter leaves in its own pixel; zoom in and it types the letters themselves.

The pictures are illustrations made by Claude from the linked reports. They are not photographs of the events.
